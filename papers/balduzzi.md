balduzzi, elton, green 2001
1. research question

how quickly do economic news announcements affect u.s. treasury prices and yields?

2.data
intraday govpx treasury data
1991 to 1995
3m, 2y, 10y, 30y securities
26 scheduled economic announcements
market expectations used to measure news surprises
3.method
event study around announcement times
compare actual releases to expected values
measure price, volume, spread, and volatility responses
4.main findings
treasury prices react extremely quickly to economic news
most significant 10y reactions occur within 1 minute
payrolls are especially important
procyclical surprises generally push bond prices down
spreads widen immediately and normalize within 5 to 15 minutes
announcements explain substantial short-term price volatility
5.important for my project
supports using 5m and 30m event windows
shows why timestamp alignment matters
establishes the baseline that new information is incorporated very quickly
my project replaces scheduled numerical announcements with continuous textual information
6.limitation
focuses on scheduled macro announcements
does not examine financial text or sentiment
no social media
older market data
