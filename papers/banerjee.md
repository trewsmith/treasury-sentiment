banerjee et al. 2024
1.research question

how does textual news sentiment affect sovereign bond yield spreads across different market regimes?

2.data
financial newspaper articles
indian sovereign bond market
monetary, fiscal, and national account news
multiple bond maturities
3.method
construct textual news sentiment measures
baseline regressions
markov regime-switching model
examine yield spreads across regimes and maturities
4.main findings
news sentiment affects sovereign yield spreads
monetary and fiscal news are particularly important
effects differ across regimes
sentiment can explain changes in the shape of the yield curve
5.important for my project
directly supports regime-dependent analysis
shows that different types of economic information can affect different parts of the curve
gives me a model for testing whether news effects change during high and low volatility periods
reinforces the need to use economically meaningful news categories rather than only positive vs negative sentiment
6.limitation
focuses on india rather than the u.s.
uses professional financial newspapers
studies yield spreads rather than intraday u.s. 10y movements
does not examine reddit or social media
