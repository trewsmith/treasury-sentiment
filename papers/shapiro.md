shapiro, sudhof, wilson 2020
1.can textual sentiment from economic news be measured accurately and used to explain economic outcomes?

2.data
238k+ newspaper articles
16 major u.s. newspapers
1980 to 2015
articles focused on u.s. economic news
3.method
compare multiple sentiment lexicons
human-rated articles used to evaluate accuracy
construct a news sentiment index
test its relationship with economic variables
4.main findings
combining lexicons improves sentiment measurement
domain-specific sentiment measures perform better
news sentiment predicts consumer sentiment
positive sentiment shocks increase consumption and output
positive sentiment also increases the real fed funds rate
effects remain after controlling for survey sentiment
5.important for my project
supports building a finance-specific sentiment measure
shows generic sentiment tools can be improved through domain adaptation
provides evidence that textual sentiment contains information beyond traditional sentiment surveys
useful foundation for testing whether financial text contains information about rates
6.limitation
focuses on broad economic sentiment rather than treasury-specific information
primarily uses daily and monthly measures
does not study intraday treasury reactions
does not compare professional news with social media
